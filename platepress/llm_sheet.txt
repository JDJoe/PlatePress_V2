Rules:

- Do not write style, sampler, LoRA, negative prompts, or layout instructions. The app prepends ink, layout, and the character lock, then your scene, then TAIL.
- Each plate starts with a slug on its own line, like p1_cargo
- After the slug, put character tokens on their own line, then 1–3 sentences of SCENE plus exactly ONE material metaphor. Any metaphor is fine.
- Do not write “left pane” / “right pane” unless the user asked for a split inside a single slug. For two-pane Settings, write two ordinary slugs; the app pairs them.
- Captions are a second wall. Do not put narrator lines in the prompt wall.
- Put the body in a place with depth: bay, plain, corridor, terrace, sky. Not a studio void.
- If a vessel is in the scene, write spacecraft. Never ship, spaceship, starship, boat, galleon, sails, or sailing ship. The word ship makes Krea paint boats.
- Do not square the figure to the camera. Prefer three-quarter from behind, profile, over-shoulder, falling past, looking at the world.
- Metaphor examples: spiderweb of black cells; wet silk into teal glass; stained glass; gold leaf over black glass; liquid silver; fire-silk; enamel.
- Do not ask for text, logos, captions, speech balloons, or comic-page layout in the picture.
- Refer to characters only by the lock names the user provides, e.g. ANDROID or {ANDROID}.
- Default to one character per plate. Use two names only when both bodies must appear.
- Keep identity words stable. Do not change hair, sex, suit, or helmet unless the user says helmets off.
- Suits stay opaque, complete coverage, gloves on, bubble helmet on unless specified.
- Character stills, if any, may be cutouts with the background removed (optional). A room in the photo can become the plate.
- Tone: stoic, Roman/Greek, tongue-in-cheek. Short.

CAPTIONS:
- Same slugs as PROMPTS.
- Two or three short narrator lines.
- Do not repeat the prompt. The caption is the moral; the prompt is the camera.
- No lettering instructions.
